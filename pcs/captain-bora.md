## Description

Dark hair, beard. Wears a brown leather jacket. Experienced, roguish — the look of someone who has made a living in the spaces between Imperial law and actual crime, and found it comfortable there.

## Background

Fought as a soldier on the Separatist side during the Clone Wars, under the command of **General Cracken**. When the war ended in defeat and the Empire rose to take the Republic's place, Bora — like a lot of veterans on the losing side — found himself with no army, no cause, and no easy way home. He and Indie ended up flying under the radar, doing whatever kept the *[Centennial Duck](../ships/centennial-duck.md)* fueled and the crew fed.

Years later, Cracken — now running a rebel cell rather than a battalion — called in the old chain of command for a personal favor: track down a man hiding on Tatooine. Bora doesn't know why Cracken picked him specifically for this job. Cracken does.

## Character Notes

Bora is the one the party looks to when things go sideways. He's good under pressure and better at talking his way into and out of things than he usually admits.

*The uncle reveal is reserved for the face-to-face at [Tusken Fort](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tusken_Fort). How Bora processes that moment — and what it means for his commitment to the mission — is one of the emotional centers of the adventure.*
