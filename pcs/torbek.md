## How He Got Here

A [Gamorrean](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamorrean/Legends) who worked collection rounds for [Jabba's](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jabba_Desilijic_Tiure/Legends) organization, squeezing protection money out of Mos Eisley's shopkeepers. On his last route, the credits went missing between the last collection and the drop-off. Not stolen at knifepoint, not gambled away. Just gone. He has no idea who took them.

Jabba doesn't accept "I don't know." Word came back fast: find the money, or don't come back at all.

Before Jabba's people could collect him personally, the Mos Eisley militia scooped him up in the chaos after the cantina ambush and threw him in a cell. No charges, no questions, just a body to process. For the first time in his life, a jail cell is the safest place a Gamorrean has ever stood. The moment he walks out those doors without answers, he's a dead man.

## The Cell

The militia threw him in with [Henry](henry.md), hoping they'd fight to the death. He took one look at a terrified adolescent Wookiee pressed into a corner and sat down. You don't finish something that's already over, that's not honor. By the time the PCs arrive, both have been quietly working the cell bars together for hours.

## GM Secret: The Bill of Sale

*Not yet revealed in play. Revealed in [Jabba's Bill](../sessions/season-2/jabbas-bill.md).*

Torbek was never Jabba's employee. **His clan sold him into slavery**, for forty crates of Gamorrean ale, and Jabba has owned him ever since. The missing collections are just Jabba's excuse. Ownership is the real hold. [Bib Fortuna](../npcs/bib-fortuna.md) has the bill of sale and will offer to sell Torbek's freedom to the crew.

This mirrors [Henry](henry.md), whom Gune rescued from slavery. Two former slaves on the same crew.
