## Description

A tall, pale [Twi'lek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek/Legends) in dark robes, his head-tails draped over one shoulder like a stole. He has long, sharp fingernails and an even sharper smile. He speaks softly and never needs to raise his voice, because everyone in the room knows who he speaks for.

## Background

[Jabba the Hutt's](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jabba_Desilijic_Tiure/Legends) majordomo, and the gatekeeper for anyone who wants an audience with the Hutt. He handles Jabba's paperwork: debts, contracts, and the bills of sale for everyone Jabba owns. When Jabba wants a debt collected politely, he sends Bib. When he wants it collected the other way, he sends Gamorreans.

**Relevant to [Torbek](../pcs/torbek.md):** Bib holds Torbek's contract, and he knows exactly what it says. See [Jabba's Bill](../sessions/season-2/jabbas-bill.md).

## Stats

## Equipment

Per the sourcebook, plus a datapad holding Torbek's contract, two [Gamorrean](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gamorrean/Legends) guards (use [Gorrt's](gorrt.md) stats).

## Personality

Silky, patient, and smug. Bib never threatens anyone directly. He describes consequences as if they were the weather. He enjoys reading contracts aloud.

> *"His Excellency is a reasonable being, Captain. He asks only for what is his."*
