# Yerkys ne Dago — Crime Boss & Loan Shark
### Neutral NPC (Recurring Complication) · Cluster-wide

← [Back to Campaign](../README.md)

---

## Description

A Twi'lek in expensive, understated clothing, always seated, always smiling. Speaks slowly, like he has nowhere else to be — because he doesn't. Two or three quiet, watchful bodyguards nearby, never obviously armed.

## Background

Escaped slavery on Ryloth young and built the largest black-market and credit network in the Minos Cluster from nothing. Most of the Cluster's tramp freighter captains — reputable or otherwise — owe him money. He is the fastest, easiest source of credits anywhere in the Cluster, which is exactly the point: he doesn't need to threaten anyone. The debt does that for him.

Gracious in person, genuinely enjoys good company and a good story, and will never be the one to raise his voice or make the threat explicit. He doesn't have to. Everyone already knows what happens to people who don't pay Yerkys ne Dago back.

## Equipment

Concealed hold-out blaster (rarely needed), 2–3 bodyguards on call (treat as seasoned bounty hunters), an extensive network of informants across the Cluster.

## Personality

Patient, warm, and utterly ruthless underneath. A debt to Yerkys is never really about the credits — it's about what he can ask for later. Good recurring pressure valve: he can call in a favor at almost any point in the season.

> *"No rush at all, my friends. We're all reasonable people here."*
