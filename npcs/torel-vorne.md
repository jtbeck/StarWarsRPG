## Description

Grey-green skin, a stiff high collar, and a counting-house manner. He speaks softly and precisely and never raises his voice. He doesn't have to. A battered ledger droid floats at his elbow and reads out balances when he asks, sometimes when he doesn't.

## Background

During the Clone Wars, Vorne was a Trade Federation logistics officer who supplied Separatist garrisons on the Rim. When the war ended and the Empire took over the Federation, Vorne bought his freedom by giving the new regime the names of his own superiors. He has been selling people to the Empire ever since. It's the one trade he has never lost money on.

He staffs his syndicate with veterans from the losing side, people like Captain Bora. They're desperate, they're good at their jobs, and nobody comes looking for them when they disappear. He calls Bora "Sergeant" to remind him which side he fought on.

## Personality

- **Everything is an account.** Favors, insults, and debts all go in the ledger, and all of them get balanced eventually.
- **He doesn't forgive debts.** Not because he's angry. He just thinks an unpaid balance is untidy.
- **He's a coward, and smart about it.** Vorne never stands in the room where the shooting happens. He hires it, sells it, or reports it.

> *"You and I lost the same war, Sergeant. The difference is that I have since been paid for it."*

## Relationship to the Crew

The crew ran jobs for Vorne until a run of ryll was lost to Imperial Customs. They kept his 10,000-credit grease fund, and he sold them out to the Empire. He doesn't know his own cargo master, [Kesk Rallo](../sessions/season-2/the-vorne-job.md#kesk-rallo--vornes-cargo-master), set the whole thing up.
