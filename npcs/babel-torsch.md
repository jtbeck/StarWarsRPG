# Babel Torsch — Imperial Customs Officer
### Neutral NPC (Recurring Complication) · Travnin, Minos Cluster

← [Back to Campaign](../README.md)

---

## Description

Precise, upright, and immaculately turned out even after a double shift. Carries a datapad he consults constantly, cross-referencing manifests against cargo by eye. Speaks softly and never raises his voice — he doesn't need to.

## Background

Formerly an aide and holo-censor on Adarlon, Torsch clawed his way out of a career-ending blackmail scandal and was reassigned to head the Imperial Office of Customs for the entire Minos Cluster — a demotion in prestige he has turned into a personal kingdom. Unlike most of the Cluster's garrison, he takes the job seriously: meticulous to the point of obsession, genuinely incorruptible, and quietly proud of being the one Imperial official out here who can't be bought. He inspects every new arrival at Travnin personally.

He isn't cruel, and he isn't looking for a fight — he's looking for paperwork that doesn't match cargo. Anyone flagged by Torsch stays flagged; he has an excellent memory and takes it personally when he's been lied to.

## Equipment

Datapad (cargo manifests, customs law), hold-out blaster (3D), comlink, standing access to a customs corvette and boarding team.

## Personality

Believes, sincerely, that he is the only thing standing between the Minos Cluster and total collapse into smuggling and piracy. Not wrong, exactly. Doesn't hate the crew — doesn't know them yet. Straight with him, he's fair. Lie and get caught, he remembers.

> *"I suggest you recalculate your load, or else you could be found guilty of defrauding the Imperial Office of Customs."*
