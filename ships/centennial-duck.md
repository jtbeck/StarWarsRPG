## Overview

The party's ship. A [Corellian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corellia/Legends) VCX-100 freighter — a sturdy, mid-range hauler known for its reliable hyperdrive and generous cargo capacity. The *Centennial Duck* has been modified over the years for speed and firepower at the expense of some cargo space. Currently docked at [Docking Bay 94](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Docking_Bay_94/Legends) in [Mos Eisley](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mos_Eisley/Legends).
