## Overview

The standard [TIE/ln starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/LN_starfighter/Legends) — cheap, mass-produced, and everywhere the Empire has a presence. Unlike the [TIE Interceptors](tie-interceptors.md) used in the Episode 8 finale, these are the Empire's baseline fighter: fast and maneuverable, but fragile, with no shields and no hyperdrive of their own. Use this stat block for any generic Imperial fighter escort, customs patrol, or Star Destroyer scramble across either season.

## Tactics

Imperial pilots fly aggressively and in numbers — a single TIE dies fast to a well-placed hit, but they rarely come alone. Squadrons favor overwhelming fire and interception runs over prolonged dogfighting; pilots are trained to press an attack rather than preserve the fighter.

**No hyperdrive:** TIE fighters can't jump on their own — they're always launched from a carrier (Star Destroyer, customs corvette, base) and can't pursue a ship that makes the jump to hyperspace.
