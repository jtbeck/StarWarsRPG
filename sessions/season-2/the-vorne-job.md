# The Vorne Job — GM Prep
### Season 2 · Flashback side quest · Told at the Oasis hearth, Day 3 evening

## Overview

**When:** The Oasis, First Twilight on Day 3. Run it after Dryon receives the crew and before [Zardra's](../../npcs/zardra.md) night attack. The attack is the hard cut that ends the flashback.
**Who's in the flashback:** Captain Bora, Indie, Eck, Gune, and Dooble. Henry, Torbek, and Graff weren't with the crew yet, so this works as a session without Torbek's player.
**Length:** About 2 hours. Four scenes, one of them a ship encounter.
**Purpose:**
1. Story: show the job that went wrong. The prologue only says it "cost Vorne a fortune" and that Vorne "sold the crew out to the Empire."
2. Economy: explain where the crew's **10,000 credits** came from. They're the money Vorne is still telling the whole Outer Rim about, and the money [Jabba's Bill](jabbas-bill.md) collects later.
3. Mechanics: a low-stakes rehearsal of the *Tramp Freighters* (WEG40095) smuggling rules before [Season 3](../season-3/the-long-way-out.md) runs on them: fees, contraband ratings, manifests, secret compartments, customs searches, bribes, and jettisoning cargo.
4. Seed: [Torel Vorne](../../npcs/torel-vorne.md) as a real face and voice, plus a traitor the crew doesn't know about yet.

## The Frame — The Hearth Toll

Torbek isn't here tonight. Old Arno died in the krayt dragon attack, and Torbek takes first watch on the ridge above the Oasis with Arno's rifle across his knees. To a Gamorrean, someone has to stand guard over a fallen guide's last night on the trail. He comes back down after the night attack is over, or in the middle of it if Torbek's player turns up.

> *After the meal, the priests bank the fire in the center of the hall. Children settle onto bantha-hair rugs. Dryon folds his hands.*
>
> *"Oasis doesn't ask for names or explanations. But travelers who sleep at our fire pay a toll. Not in credits. A story. Something true, that you carried here with you."*

If the players need a nudge, point them at the supply crate the priests use as a bench by the fire. It held vaporator parts, bought last month from "a Duros trader." Stenciled on its side is a mark the crew knows: a **closed hand around a coin**, the sigil of the Vorne syndicate. A young priest notices them staring and asks what it means.

Have the players decide who starts telling the story. Then cut.

> *Eighteen months ago. Ord Mantell. Rain on the windscreen, and a message from a man who never asks twice.*

**Henry** was not there. Have him curl up next to Gune and listen. Partway through he asks (through Gune) whether this was "before me." It was. The crew was on the run from this job when they found him.

## Flashback Rules

Read these to the table before the first scene.

**Fixed points.** Everybody knows how this ends. Players can change anything except these three things:
1. **The cargo never reaches the buyer.**
2. **The crew walks away with 10,000 of Vorne's credits.**
3. **Vorne blames the crew and sells them to the Empire.**

The fun is in *how*. A bad roll doesn't break the story. It makes it a funnier one.

**"That's not how it happened."** Each player gets **one** of these. After any failed roll, the player interrupts the storyteller, re-tells the moment their way, and rerolls. Memories get generous around a fire. The Dim-U kids love it when the story changes.

**Who's at the table:**

| Character | Status |
|---|---|
| Captain Bora, Indie, Eck, Gune | Played. Use current sheets minus anything picked up on Tatooine. |
| Dooble | Along for the job. NPC, or hand him to whoever wants a second character. |
| Henry | Not there yet. Listening at the fire. |
| Torbek | Not there yet. On watch on the ridge. |
| Graff Raster | Not there. **His player can guest-star as [Kesk Rallo](#kesk-rallo--vornes-cargo-master)**, Vorne's minder on the job. See the secret card below. |

**Wounds** taken in the flashback don't carry over. **Character Points** spent in the flashback stay spent. Award CP at the end of the session as normal.

## Scene One — Vorne's Offer

**Location:** A gutted Separatist troop barge, beached in the scrapyards outside Worlport on [Ord Mantell](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Mantell/Legends). It's Vorne's office.

> *The barge still has its Confederacy markings, painted over badly. Inside, it's warm and dry and lit like a counting house. A Neimoidian in a stiff collar sits behind a desk built from a blast door, a ledger droid at his elbow. He doesn't stand.*
>
> *"Sergeant Hue. Sit. You and I lost the same war. The difference is that I have since been paid for it."*

[Torel Vorne](../../npcs/torel-vorne.md) wants **one metric ton of [ryll](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ryll_kor/Legends)** run to a buyer at Tessal Deep, a mining station in an asteroid belt three jumps rimward. Imperial Customs has been busy on the direct routes. That's why he's hiring the crew instead of using his own ships.

**The goods:** Ryll is **X (Illegal)**. Possession alone can mean an on-the-spot sentence from an Imperial Customs officer (*Tramp Freighters* p. 22). One ton is about 1 cubic meter of sealed canisters. At the base price of 35,000 cr/ton, black-market resale runs several times that. To Vorne it's a fortune.

### Mechanic: Bargaining the Fee

Smugglers get paid for risk, not tonnage. A legitimate hauler would get **5–10 cr per ton per day** (*Tramp Freighters* p. 10). Vorne opens at **2,000 cr**. *Half up front, half on delivery* is standard (p. 11).

| Result of opposed *bargain* (crew vs. Vorne 7D) | Fee |
|---|---|
| Vorne wins by 5+ | 1,500 cr, and he looks pleased about it |
| Vorne wins | 2,000 cr |
| Crew wins | 2,500 cr |
| Crew wins by 5+ | 3,500 cr |

The fee doesn't change any fixed point. The crew never collects the back half. Whatever they bargain for, they bank the up-front half.

### The Grease Fund

Vorne slides a credstick across the desk. **10,000 credits.**

> *"For Customs. Every Imperial out there has a price, and most of them are cheap. You spend what you must. You return what you don't. Every credit is in the ledger." The droid chirps.*

**Ask the table who carries it.** That player decides what happens to it in Scene Four. (This is fixed point #2, and the 10k on the [Crew Ledger](../../README.md#crew-ledger).)

### Mechanic: Secret Compartment

Vorne's mechanics will fit the *Centennial Duck* with a **Hidden** compartment for the ryll at Vorne's expense. *"Consider it an investment in our future."* (*Tramp Freighters* p. 42.)

| Grade | To find by eye | To find by scanner | Cost per ton hidden |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concealed | Difficult *Perception* | Found automatically | 200 cr |
| **Hidden** *(this one)* | Very Difficult *Perception* | Moderate *Perception* | 400 cr |
| Scanner Resistant | Very Difficult *Perception* | Doesn't show up | 1,000 cr |

Each ton hidden costs **5 tons** of cargo space. If more than 20% of a ship's hold goes to compartments, customs gets suspicious just from the math. One ton of ryll uses 5 of the *Duck's* 100 tons, so this one is fine.

**Gune** can supervise the install. On a Moderate *space transports repair* or *Perception*, he notices one of Vorne's mechanics fit something extra into the compartment lining: a **pulse beacon**, the kind you'd use to find a crate floating in space. Vorne's people say it's standard: *"In case you have to drop it. We'd want it back."* That's true. It's also how [Kesk](#kesk-rallo--vornes-cargo-master) plans to steal it.

> **Optional, recommended:** let the compartment survive the flashback. The *Duck* still has it in the present day, a 1-ton Hidden space nobody has used since. It's useful when [Babel Torsch](../../npcs/babel-torsch.md) boards them at Travnin. If you keep it, add it to the [ship sheet](../../ships/centennial-duck.md).

### Mechanic: The Cover Cargo and the Manifest

A ship running empty to a mining station draws attention. The crew needs **legitimate cargo** and a **manifest** that matches it.

**Find cover cargo.** One crew member makes a *streetwise* or *Perception* roll around the Worlport docks (*Tramp Freighters* p. 10):

| Roll | Cover cargo found |
|---|---|
| Fail | Nothing. They fly empty (+5 to Customs' suspicion in Scene Three) |
| Very Easy | Hydroponic nutrient paste. Low pay, nobody cares |
| Easy / Moderate | Mining drill bits for Tessal Deep: 40 tons, 1,200 cr on delivery. Perfect cover |
| Difficult+ | Medical supplies for Tessal Deep, 60 tons, 2,500 cr. Perfect cover, and Customs waves medical runs through faster (−5 to their suspicion) |

**Forge the manifest.** *forgery* roll. Whatever the total is, write it down. It's the difficulty Customs has to beat in Scene Three to notice the manifest is wrong.

## Scene Two — The Run

> *Ord Mantell falls away behind you. Kesk Rallo has taken the jump seat behind the pilot without asking, long grey fingers already on the nav console.*
>
> *"Vorne prefers the Kreel Spiral. Quieter."*

**[Kesk Rallo](#kesk-rallo--vornes-cargo-master)** is Vorne's cargo master. He rides along to guard Vorne's goods. He has his own plan.

### Mechanic: Choosing a Route

*Tramp Freighters* (p. 24): on popular routes, the chance of meeting Imperial Customs is about **1 in 20**. Smugglers take the longer, quieter ones.

| Route | Time | Astrogation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main trade lane | 9 hours | Easy | Busy. The listed 1 in 20 Customs chance. |
| **Kreel Spiral** *(Kesk's choice)* | 14 hours | Moderate | "Quiet." Kesk knows exactly who's waiting there. |
| Indie's own route through the Tessaline drift | 20 hours | Difficult | Nobody patrols it. Failure means a hyperdrive mishap: roll on the Minor Hyperdrive Mishap table (*Tramp Freighters* p. 91). |

**It doesn't matter which route they take.** Customs is waiting at the end of the Spiral. If the crew picks another route, Kesk quietly sends their new heading by burst transmission halfway through the trip, and the customs ship catches them on the way out of hyperspace.

**Clue:** anyone at the comms or sensors who makes a Moderate *communications* or *sensors* roll catches the outgoing burst. Kesk says it's a coded check-in with Vorne. *"He likes to know where his property is."* That's believable enough.

### Downtime Beat

Use the long trip for character moments. Prompt each player with one question:
- **Bora:** Vorne called you "Sergeant." What did Vorne do in the war that you don't like to think about?
- **Indie:** Why didn't you want to take this job?
- **Eck:** Kesk keeps sitting with his back to the wall. What does that tell you about him?
- **Gune:** What else did you find in the compartment that you haven't mentioned yet?

## Scene Three — Customs

> *You drop out of hyperspace and the proximity alarm screams. Dead ahead, close enough to read the hull numbers: an Imperial Customs corvette, already turning its tractor projector toward you.*
>
> *"Freighter Centennial Duck. This is Lieutenant Arvel Doss, Imperial Office of Customs. Cut your engines and prepare to be boarded."*

Lieutenant Doss knows the ship's name before he scans it. Anyone who catches that (Easy *Perception*) knows somebody tipped him off.

The crew has four options, and they'll probably try more than one. **Every path ends with the cargo lost**, and with Kesk quietly getting it.

### Option A: Comply and Bluff

Doss boards with **six customs troopers**.

- **The manifest:** Doss rolls *Perception* 4D+1 against the crew's *forgery* total. Apply the cover-cargo modifiers from Scene One.
- **The search:** Doss's troopers sweep with a hand scanner, which finds the Hidden compartment on a Moderate *Perception*. Before they even roll, Doss walks straight to the right bulkhead panel. **He knew where to look.** Anyone watching notices on an Easy *Perception*.
- **The con:** A good story (opposed *con* against Doss's *Perception*) gets the crew an argument instead of an arrest: *"Prove you knew it was there."* It doesn't save the cargo.

**Outcome:** Doss "seizes" the ryll and fines the ship 2,000 cr for the paperwork. On a total failure, he impounds the ship, and the crew has to talk or shoot their way out. Fixed point #1 holds. The ryll never reaches a Customs evidence locker. Doss and Kesk split it later.

### Option B: Bribe

This is what the grease fund is for. Bribing an Imperial officer is an opposed *bargain* or *con* against Doss's *bargain* 4D.

**Doss turns it down. All of it.** Even the full 10,000. *"Put that away before I add it to the charges."* A customs lieutenant on the Rim turning down 10,000 credits makes no sense, unless someone has already paid him more. Let the players work that out. Kesk insists that Doss "must be one of the honest ones."

The credstick stays with the crew.

### Option C: Run

The corvette has a tractor beam and better guns, but the *Duck* is faster.

| Step | Roll |
|---|---|
| Break the tractor lock | Opposed *space transports* vs. tractor beam 4D. Each failed round, the corvette drags the *Duck* closer. |
| Get clear to jump | Two successful rounds of opposed *space transports* (*Duck* Space 6 vs. corvette Space 5). |
| Plot the jump under fire | Moderate *astrogation*, +10 if they rush it into a single round. |
| Lose weight | **Jettison the cargo.** With the ryll gone, the *Duck* gets +1D to escape and Doss stops to "secure the evidence." |

*Jettisoning:* the crew has to open the compartment and blow it out by hand (Kesk "helps"). An automatic jettison system costs 1,000 cr and isn't installed yet (*Tramp Freighters* p. 42). That's a good upgrade to sell them in Season 3.

**Outcome:** The ryll tumbles off into the asteroid field, and the pulse beacon from Scene One starts transmitting. Kesk's people pick it up hours later. If Gune found the beacon, the players may now realize who it was really for.

### Option D: Fight

It's a bad idea, and a fun one. The corvette is tougher than the *Duck*, and the next corvette is about an hour out. After two rounds of combat, Doss's crew blows the cargo bay open with a lucky shot (or a planted charge: Kesk again). The ryll is lost in the debris.

### Lt. Arvel Doss — Imperial Customs Officer

Pinched, tidy, and greedy. He enjoys the regulations as much as the money.

| DEX | KNO | MEC | PER | STR | TEC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2D+2 | 3D | 2D+1 | 3D+1 | 2D+2 | 2D |

Blaster 4D, bureaucracy 6D, law enforcement 5D, bargain 4D, *Perception* 4D+1 (searches only), command 4D. Blaster pistol (4D).

### Customs Troopers ×6

All stats 2D except DEX 3D. Blaster 4D, brawling 3D. Blaster rifle (5D), hand scanner.

## Scene Four — The Ledger

Kesk gets more urgent the moment the cargo is gone.

> *"We go back to Vorne. Now. We tell him exactly what happened, and you give him back his grease money, every credit. That's the only way any of us survives this."*

Kesk really does want the 10,000 back. He'd skim it before it reached Vorne. If anyone pushes, he gets nervous and a little too insistent.

**Vorne's holo call** comes in before they've decided:

> *The ledger droid's voice first, flat and polite: "Outstanding balance: one metric ton, ryll. Ten thousand credits, liquid." Then Vorne himself, very calm.*
>
> *"Sergeant. I am told my property is gone. I do not care how. I care that the account balances. Bring me the ten thousand and we will discuss the rest of what you owe me. Like gentlemen."*
>
> *He leans closer to the pickup.*
>
> *"Or keep it. And learn what it costs."*

**The player holding the credstick decides.** Fixed point #2 says they keep it. Let them own the reason: they don't trust Vorne, they know they were set up, it's the only money they have, or Bora's pride.

### Kesk's Exit

If Kesk is still aboard when the crew keeps the money, he asks to be dropped at the next port and the crew never sees him again. If the crew has figured him out, he talks fast: *"Vorne will kill me too if he hears you say it."* He's right. Whether they let him walk or space him is up to the players. A living Kesk is more useful later. See [Hooks](#hooks).

### The Sell-Out

Fixed point #3. Within the week, Vorne gives Imperial Customs the *Duck's* transponder code, the crew's faces, and a sworn statement naming them as ryll smugglers. Lieutenant Doss is happy to confirm it. That's how the crew ended up running, and how they ended up near the Kessel lanes the week they found Henry.

## Cut Back — The Oasis

End the flashback on Vorne's line, or on whatever the crew does with the credstick. Then cut straight to the present without a break:

> *The fire pops. The children are asleep in a pile against a bantha's flank. Dryon is quiet for a long moment.*
>
> *"You carry a debt that isn't yours, and money that is. The Bantha would say —"*
>
> *The window behind him shatters. A smoke grenade bounces across the floor.*

## Kesk Rallo — Vorne's Cargo Master

A [Duros](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duros/Legends) spacer, long and quiet, with a grey flight coat and a habit of tapping on whatever's nearest him. He has worked for Vorne for nine years and has been stealing from him for six.

| DEX | KNO | MEC | PER | STR | TEC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3D | 2D+2 | 3D+2 | 3D | 2D+1 | 3D |

Blaster 4D+1, dodge 4D, streetwise 4D, astrogation 5D, space transports 5D, con 5D, sneak 4D, computer programming/repair 4D, security 4D. Hold-out blaster (3D+2), datapad, a second comlink he never mentions.

### Secret Card — For Graff's Player

*Hand this over privately if Graff's player guest-stars as Kesk.*

> **You are Kesk Rallo.** You're going to steal Vorne's ryll, and these smugglers are going to take the blame.
>
> - Lieutenant Arvel Doss of Imperial Customs is waiting at the end of the Kreel Spiral. You've paid him. He gets half.
> - If the crew takes a different route, send Doss their heading. Do it quietly.
> - If they jettison the cargo, the beacon in the compartment will lead your people to it. If Doss seizes it, he hands it to you later.
> - You want the 10,000-credit grease fund back too, so you can skim it on the way to Vorne.
> - **Don't get caught.** If they work it out, survive. Lie, beg, make a deal.
>
> Your goal isn't to beat the other players. Your goal is to make them believe this was bad luck. You're allowed to play to lose.

Graff in the present day knows nothing about any of this. If the player would rather not keep a secret from the table, the GM runs Kesk instead and the scenes play the same way.

## Mechanics Introduced — Quick Reference

*All from Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters (WEG40095). Page numbers are the book's own.*

| Mechanic | Rule | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Drop-point fees | 5–10 cr per ton per day; half up front, half on delivery | 10–11 |
| Finding cargo | *streetwise* or *Perception*; difficulty sets run length and profit | 10 |
| Contraband ratings | **F** (fee/permit), **R** (restricted, license), **X** (illegal) | 22 |
| Black-market prices | Marketeer sells at ×2 legal / ×3 F / ×4 R / ×5 X; buys at ×0.5 / ×1.5 / ×2 / ×2.5 | 91 |
| Finding the black market | *streetwise* vs. Black Market Contact Table; +1 difficulty step per extra day | 19, 93 |
| Secret compartments | Concealed / Hidden / Scanner Resistant; 5 tons of space per ton hidden; more than 20% of the hold looks suspicious | 42 |
| Automatic cargo jettison | 1,000 cr, 1 ton | 42 |
| Customs on busy routes | ~1 in 20 chance per trip | 24 |
| Sensor decoys / jammers | +2D to tell which reading is the real ship / −2D to detect | 41 |
| Luxury goods | Ryll 35,000 cr/ton (1 m³/ton); spice 2,700 cr/ton | 92 |

## Hooks

| Hook | Where it can go |
|---|---|
| **Kesk is on Tatooine** | The Vorne-marked crate the Dim-U priest asked about came from "a Duros trader, last month." If you want it, Kesk is working Mos Eisley now and could turn up in Episode 8, or be bought out by [Jabba's](jabbas-bill.md) people. |
| **Lt. Arvel Doss** | Customs officers get transferred. A Doss promoted into the Minos Cluster would make a good rival for [Babel Torsch](../../npcs/babel-torsch.md): a crooked officer next to an honest one. |
| **Clearing the crew's name** | If the crew ever proves to Vorne that Kesk stole his ryll, Vorne might forgive the cargo. He won't forgive the 10,000. |
| **The compartment** | A 1-ton Hidden compartment that's still in the *Duck*. See Scene One. |
| **Vorne's ledger** | Vorne never forgets an outstanding balance. After [Jabba's Bill](jabbas-bill.md) takes their money, he'll still want his 10k. That works as a Season 3 threat. |
