## Overview

**What it is:** The Docking Bay 94 service droid presents the *Centennial Duck*'s bill for docking and resupply. It's larger than it should be, because the ship has been feeding, watering and breathing for **one more person than the crew manifest lists**.

**The secret:** [Gune](../../pcs/gune.md) kidnapped an Imperial officer on Kwenn Space Station and has been hiding him aboard the *Duck* ever since. Captain Bora doesn't know. *(See [The Stowaway](#the-stowaway) below.)*

**Purpose:**
1. Drain more of the [Vorne 10k](../../README.md#crew-ledger) that Indie stole from [Torel Vorne](../../npcs/torel-vorne.md), in a way that comes from the story and isn't just a toll.
2. Plant a clue about the officer that the crew can solve with alibis.
3. Let the table blame [Henry](../../pcs/henry.md) and [Torbek](../../pcs/torbek.md) first, because a Wookiee and a Gamorrean really do eat that much. Then let the timestamps clear them.

**Two parts:**

| Part | When | What the crew learns |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The Invoice** | Dawn of Day 3, when the crew regroups at the *Duck* after [the Jailbreak](jailbreak.md) and before they meet Old Arno | The ship used too much food, water and waste processing. Some odd room-service orders came from Hold 2. **Easy to blame Henry and Torbek.** |
| **2. The Addendum** | Episode 8, when they return to Docking Bay 94 (just before or after [Jabba's Bill](jabbas-bill.md)) | Hold 2 kept breathing while **every crew member was out in the Wastes.** |

## The Rules Underneath

The bill follows real rules:

- **Landing permit:** 20 cr/day (*Tatooine Manhunt*, p. 8).
- **Automatic restock:** Standard-class ports and better restock and service every landed ship within one planetary day and **charge for it automatically unless the captain declines** (*Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters*, p. 30). Nobody declined.
- **Restock formula:** `base fee × total crew + passenger capacity × days of consumables renewed` (*Tramp Freighters*, p. 31). Mos Eisley is cheap by Outer Rim standards, so use a **20 cr** base fee (the range is 10–35). The *Duck* carries 6 crew and 6 passengers, for a capacity of **12**.

The formula charges by **capacity**, not by headcount, so the extra person doesn't change the standard restock. He shows up in the **excess-draw** lines instead. The bay meters the ship's actual usage and bills anything over the rated draw. This is where the clues are.

## Part 1 — The Invoice (Dawn, Day 3)

> *A squat service droid is waiting by the* Duck*'s landing gear. It's dented, sand-scoured and painted in faded Docking Bay 94 orange, with a datapad clamped in one manipulator. It rolls toward Captain Bora and chimes politely.*
>
> *"Good morning, Captain. Your vessel has received the standard restock and maintenance package, as you did not decline it. Your itemized statement is ready. Payment is due before departure clearance. Docking Bay 94 thanks you for your patronage and reminds you that the Mynock netting is not included."*

### The Statement

| Line | Item | Charge | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Landing permit, 4 days × 20 cr | 80 cr | The whole stay, billed up front. |
| 2 | Standard restock, 20 base × 12 capacity × 4 days | 960 cr | Posted rate. Not negotiable. |
| 3 | Excess draw: food-converter protein base, **2.5× rated** | 120 cr | **Red herring.** Blame Henry. |
| 4 | Excess draw: potable water, **3× rated** | 60 cr | **Red herring.** Wookiee grooming? |
| 5 | Waste reclamation surcharge: *"biological load exceeds rated occupancy"* | 90 cr | **Red herring.** Blame Torbek. |
| 6 | Service-panel orders, **Cargo Hold 2** | 45 cr | **The clue.** See below. |
| | **Total** | **1,355 cr** | |

**Line 6, read out in full if anyone asks:**

| Time | Order | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Day 2, 0910 | Caf, premium Core Worlds roast, ×2 | 12 cr |
| Day 2, 1430 | Boot polish, Imperial black, 1 tin | 8 cr |
| Day 2, 2350 | Hygiene kit, human, ×1 | 15 cr |
| Day 2, 2355 | Garment pressing service, ×1. *"Note: customer specified 'regulation creases.'"* | 10 cr |

> *The droid adds, helpfully: "The Hold 2 occupant has also asked for a copy of the* Imperial Navy Code of Conduct*. We do not stock it. I have marked the request 'pending.'"*

Use that last line only if the table is slow to engage. It gives the game away quickly.

### Who Gets Blamed (and the Alibis That Clear Them)

Let the table go. The fun is the crew working out the timestamps on their own.

| Suspect | Why they look guilty | What clears them |
|---|---|---|
| **[Henry](../../pcs/henry.md)** | A growing adolescent Wookiee eats a lot, and the fur needs water. Lines 3 and 4 make sense for him. | He was in a **jail cell** from the cantina ambush onward. He couldn't have placed the 2350 and 2355 orders. |
| **[Torbek](../../pcs/torbek.md)** | A Gamorrean. Line 5 writes itself. Also, why would he want boot polish? | He was in the **same cell** as Henry. Neither of them could reach a service panel. |
| **[Eck](../../pcs/eck.md)** | The best comedy suspect. Eck **stayed outside during the jailbreak** (see his rap sheet), so he's the only one without an alibi for the late-night orders. | Nothing in Part 1 clears him. Eck stays a suspect until Part 2, which is exactly the point. |
| **[Gune](../../pcs/gune.md)** | He's the engineer and nobody knows the ship better. But he was inside the police station picking locks. | His alibi for 2350 is solid. **The orders were placed by the officer, not by Gune.** Gune's alibi is real, and it means nothing. |

**The logic the crew can reach:** The late-night Hold 2 orders were placed while Henry, Torbek, Gune, Bora and Indie were all at the police station. Either Eck did it or **someone else is aboard.**

### Gune's Options

Gune's player knows the truth. Give them room to play it. Check privately before the session, or slip them a note when the droid rolls up, to see whether they want to sweat it out or come clean.

| Gune tries to... | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Steer blame** at Henry or Torbek | Free. Let him. Henry's wounded howl is its own reward. |
| **Pay it quietly** | Gune can cover lines 3–6 (315 cr) out of his personal funds before Bora reads the bill closely. That needs an opposed *con* vs. Bora's *Perception*, or Indie's if she's looking over his shoulder. If it works, Bora only sees lines 1–2. |
| **Slice the droid** and wipe the Hold 2 entries | Moderate *computer programming/repair*. **Success:** line 6 disappears and lines 3–5 are blamed on "meter calibration," which saves 315 cr. **Failure:** the droid flags it as tampering, adds a **100 cr** audit fee, and reads every deleted line out loud. |
| **Come clean** | Great. Skip to the confrontation in [The Stowaway](#the-stowaway). The rest of the bill is still owed. |

**Disputing the bill:** The droid won't haggle. Lines 1 and 2 are posted rates. If the crew disputes lines 3–5, it pulls up the meter logs, which **show the extra draw coming from Hold 2.** Every argument the crew makes produces another clue.

**Paying:** The droid accepts payment now or holds it until departure. Most crews say *"later"* because Arno is leaving at dawn. Either way, it goes on the ledger.

## Part 2 — The Addendum (Episode 8)

When the crew gets back to Docking Bay 94 on the night of Day 4, the *Duck* has **docking clamps** on its landing struts. [Jabba's Bill](jabbas-bill.md) opens with the clamps, then Bib Fortuna walks in. When Bib leaves, his last line is *"Do pay your bills."* That's the droid's cue. Keep this short. The stormtroopers are coming.

> *The orange droid rolls out of the shadows and chimes. "Welcome back, Captain. An addendum has been added to your statement. The docking clamps will be released once it is settled."*

| Line | Item | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Excess draw, Cargo Hold 2 life support: **continuous occupancy, Day 3 0600 through Day 4 2100** | 120 cr |
| 8 | Service-panel orders, Hold 2: caf ×4, *"stern note to management"* ×1 (no charge) | 24 cr |
| 9 | Late-payment fee, if they deferred in Part 1 | 25 cr |
| | **Addendum total** | **144–169 cr** |

**The clue:** For two days the *entire* crew was in the Wastes with Old Arno, Eck included. Hold 2 kept breathing anyway. **Someone is on this ship.**

> *"Shall I continue billing the Hold 2 passenger separately, Captain? He has asked me to tell you that he 'demands to speak to whoever is in charge of this vessel.'"*

**If they still don't get it,** the officer bangs on the inside of the Hold 2 hatch with the boot-polish tin.

**The clamps:** Bay 94's clamps and the control-tower handshake stay locked until the whole statement (Part 1 plus the addendum) is paid. The crew can pay, or they can slice the clamps (Difficult *computer programming/repair*, with stormtroopers incoming) and leave **without clearance.** That adds **+1 Mos Eisley Heat** to Captain Bora as the registered owner. Either choice works for the story.

## The Stowaway

> **⚠ Placeholder name.** He isn't named anywhere in the notes yet. Rename him freely.

**Lt. Tarrik Moll** — junior Imperial officer, Kwenn Space Station logistics.

- **Most likely the officer whose uniform Eck and Gune wore** to get through Kwenn ([Prologue, Episode 1](../prologue-recap.md)). Gune needed the uniform and couldn't leave a witness behind, so he took the whole officer. If you use this, he got his uniform back *eventually*, which explains the pressing service.
- Has been stowed in Cargo Hold 2 since Day 1. He's fed, watered, and getting more indignant by the hour. Gune has been treating him more like a difficult houseguest than a prisoner.
- Uses the service panel the way he'd use room service at an Imperial officers' hotel. He has no idea that each order goes on the captain's bill.
- **Why he matters:** Kwenn's quarantine and the *Relentless* both came out of Kwenn. A missing logistics officer means someone is looking for him. He might know the *Relentless*'s repair schedule, crew rosters or patrol codes. He's useful to Cracken's cell, and dangerous to keep.

## Ledger

| Item | Typical |
|---|---|
| Part 1 statement | −1,355 (or −1,040 if Gune slices lines 3–6 away) |
| Part 2 addendum | −144 to −169 |
| **Total** | **≈ −1,500** |

**Watch the balance.** 10,000 − ~7,500 (Jabba's typical bill) − ~1,500 (this) leaves about **1,000 cr**. If they also pay Depp's 2,200 to clear their Mos Eisley Heat, they **can't cover everything.** Something has to give: bargain Bib down toward the 6,500 floor, skip Depp, or slice out without clearance. That's good pressure on the way into [The Long Way Out](../season-3/the-long-way-out.md).
