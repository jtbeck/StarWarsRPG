## Overview

**When:** When the crew gets back to the *Centennial Duck* at [Docking Bay 94](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Docking_Bay_94/Legends) during the run back through Mos Eisley in Episode 8. Run it before the stormtroopers and [IG-72](../../npcs/ig-72.md) arrive, so the clock is already ticking.
**Who:** [Bib Fortuna](../../npcs/bib-fortuna.md) and two Gamorrean guards. They walk in right after the crew finds the docking clamps on their ship.
**Scene order:**
1. **The clamps.** The crew gets back to Bay 94 and finds the *Duck* clamped to the deck.
2. **Bib enters** and presents Jabba's bill. They deal with it.
3. **Bib's exit line:** *pay your bills.*
4. **The droid's addendum.** The bay's service droid rolls out with the rest of the docking statement ([The Resupply Bill, Part 2](the-resupply-bill.md#part-2--the-addendum-episode-8)). The clamps release once it's paid.
**Purpose:**
1. Consequence: the jailbreak was loud, and Jabba found out that Torbek is loose.
2. Reveal: Torbek was never hired. His clan **sold** him.
3. Economy: the crew is carrying about **10,000 credits** that Indie stole from Torel Vorne, and they need to arrive in the Minos Cluster broke. This scene takes most of it.
4. Secret: **Bib is running his own grift.** The money is going into his pocket, not Jabba's. See below.

## How Bib Found Out

The jailbreak set off the alarm, and by morning all of Mos Eisley was talking about it: a Wookiee and a *Gamorrean* broken out of the police station. [Akkik](../../npcs/akkik.md) heard the gossip and passed it up to Bib within the hour. Bib's people have been watching Docking Bay 94 ever since. They didn't need to chase the crew into the Wastes. Everyone has to come back to their ship eventually.

**How Bib knows they can pay:** [Torel Vorne](../../npcs/torel-vorne.md) has been telling everyone in the Outer Rim that this crew robbed him of ten thousand credits. Hutts and their majordomos listen to that kind of gossip. Bib's price matches what the crew has.

## GM Secret: Bib's Grift

*Players shouldn't learn this here. Let it surface later, or never.*

**Akkik took the money.** The collections that went missing on Torbek's last route were stolen by [Akkik](../../npcs/akkik.md), who let the Gamorrean take the blame. That's the answer to Torbek's "who took the credits?" hook.

**Bib knows, and he finds it funny.** He hasn't told Jabba and doesn't plan to. A Jawa who steals from a Hutt and frames a Gamorrean is useful to have in his pocket, and it gives Bib a hold over him.

**Jabba will never see this money.** Bib is collecting in His Excellency's name and pocketing every credit. Jabba has written off the missing collections as a rounding error. He doesn't know Torbek is loose, or doesn't care.

**Torbek means nothing to Bib.** The bill of sale is real, but to Bib it's just a piece of paper that happens to be worth a few thousand credits today. He feels no anger, takes nothing personally, and has no interest in the Gamorrean beyond the sale. Play it that way: Bib barely looks at Torbek. He talks to whoever holds the money.

**How it shows at the table:**
- When the "lost collections" come up, Bib is a little *too* amused. If Torbek protests that he didn't take them, Bib says: *"Oh, I'm quite sure you didn't."* and moves on.
- Opposed *Perception* vs. Bib's *con*: on a success, the character notices that Bib finds the missing money **funny**, which a loyal majordomo shouldn't. They can't prove anything, but they know Bib knows something.
- If anyone mentions Akkik by name, Bib's smile widens a fraction. *"Our little friend? A diligent creature. Very diligent."*

## The Scene

### The Clamps

> *The docking bay doors grind open. The* Centennial Duck *sits where you left it, but something is wrong. Heavy orange docking clamps are bolted around all three landing struts, and a fat cable runs from the hull to a lockbox on the bay wall. A red light on the lockbox blinks slowly. The* Duck *isn't going anywhere.*

Let the crew react. Someone will go for the clamps, someone will go for the lockbox, and Gune will start reaching for tools. Give them a round or two, then:

### Bib Enters

> *Footsteps on the stairs behind you, unhurried. A tall, pale Twi'lek in dark robes descends into the bay, head-tails draped over one shoulder, a Gamorrean with a vibroaxe on either side of him. He looks at the clamps, then at you, and smiles with too many teeth.*
>
> *"Ah. The heroes of the Mos Eisley police station. Having a little trouble with your departure?" He doesn't wait for an answer. "I am Bib Fortuna. I speak for His Excellency, Jabba the Hutt. And I am afraid you have something of his."*

**The clamps are not Bib's.** They belong to the bay, for the unpaid docking statement. Bib didn't put them there, but he's happy to let the crew assume he did. If they ask, he only says: *"I'm sure it's nothing that can't be settled."*

### The Bill

Bib produces a datapad and reads from it in a pleasant, unhurried voice.

| Item | Bib's asking price | Floor after bargaining | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lost day's collections | 2,500 cr | **2,500 cr** | Not negotiable. *"It is His Excellency's money. Whether the Gamorrean lost it or ate it is immaterial."* |
| Buyout of Torbek's contract | 6,000 cr | **4,000 cr** | See the Reveal below. |
| "Inconvenience to His Excellency" | 1,000 cr | **0 cr** | Bib's padding. He gives it up if the crew bargains well. |
| **Total** | **9,500 cr** | **6,500 cr** | |

**Bargaining:** Opposed *bargain* (whoever negotiates) vs. Bib's *bargain* (stats: *Star Wars Trilogy Sourcebook*, p. 67). For each step of success, take about 1,000 cr off the total, down to the 6,500 floor. A good argument (Torbek's years of service, the Empire about to land, the crew already being *in* the docking bay) gives the negotiator +1D. Threatening Bib gives +1D to *Bib*, because he enjoys it.

**Typical outcome:** about **7,500 cr**.

### The Reveal

When the crew asks why they should pay for Torbek's "contract," Bib smiles and reads it aloud.

> *"Bill of sale. One Gamorrean, male, of fighting age, transferred in full to the household of Jabba Desilijic Tiure… purchased from the clan of his birth…"* He glances up. *"For forty crates of Gamorrean ale. They drove a hard bargain, I'm told."*

Torbek was never an employee. His clan **sold him into slavery**, and Jabba has owned him ever since. The missing collections were only an excuse to come after him. The ownership was always the real hold.

**Let it land.** Henry was also rescued from slavery (by Gune). Play that parallel for all it's worth: Henry may move to stand next to Torbek without being asked.

## Outcomes

### They Pay
- Bib hands over the datapad. The bill of sale is now **Torbek's own**. He can destroy it, keep it, or give it to whoever paid.
- **Torbek is free.** For a Gamorrean, being bought out of bondage creates a serious debt of honor. Decide with the Torbek player who he feels he owes.
- Bib bows and turns for the stairs. See [Bib's Exit](#bibs-exit).
- Record the payment in the [Crew Ledger](../../README.md#crew-ledger) and on [Torbek's Rap Sheet](../../pcs/torbek.md#rap-sheet).

### They Pay Only the Collections (2,500 cr)
- Bib pockets it and considers the matter settled. Torbek is still **property**, and now a runaway.
- Bib doesn't care about Torbek, but he does care about a bill of sale he couldn't sell. He posts a bounty in Jabba's name (suggested 3,000 cr, alive) and keeps the bill of sale. Raise Torbek's Heat with **Jabba's organization** to ●●○ 2. Hutt contacts reach the Minos Cluster eventually.

### They Refuse Entirely
- Bib doesn't fight. He nods, steps aside, and the Gamorreans plant themselves at the bay doors.
- In the next scene, Bib sells the crew's location to the Imperials and pockets that fee instead. The stormtroopers arrive early and in larger numbers. Raise everyone's Mos Eisley Heat by one, and Torbek gets the Jabba bounty above.

### They Start Shooting
- Bib is the first one out the door. His Gamorreans cover his retreat.
- Same as refusing, except Jabba's bounty now covers the **whole crew** (500 cr each). Torbek's bounty is 5,000 cr, alive. Bib takes being shot at personally. It's the one thing in this scene that he does.

## Bib's Exit

However it ends (unless he's running from blaster fire), Bib pauses at the foot of the stairs and looks back at the clamps.

> *"Oh, and Captain…" He nods at the blinking lockbox. "Do pay your bills. Mos Eisley is* such *a small town for people who don't."*

He's gone before anyone can answer. **Right on cue, the bay's orange service droid rolls out of the shadows** with the addendum to the docking statement. Go straight to [The Resupply Bill, Part 2](the-resupply-bill.md#part-2--the-addendum-episode-8). The clamps release when it's paid.
