## Overview

**When:** When the crew gets back to the *Centennial Duck* at [Docking Bay 94](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Docking_Bay_94/Legends) during the run back through Mos Eisley in Episode 8. Run it before the stormtroopers and [IG-72](../../npcs/ig-72.md) arrive, so the clock is already ticking.
**Who:** [Bib Fortuna](../../npcs/bib-fortuna.md) and two Gamorrean guards are waiting at the foot of the boarding ramp.
**Purpose:**
1. Consequence: the jailbreak was loud, and Jabba found out that Torbek is loose.
2. Reveal: Torbek was never hired. His clan **sold** him.
3. Economy: the crew is carrying about **10,000 credits** stolen from Torel Vorne, and they need to arrive in the Minos Cluster broke. This scene takes most of it.

---

## How Jabba Found Out

The jailbreak set off the alarm, and by morning all of Mos Eisley was talking about it: a Wookiee and a *Gamorrean* broken out of the police station. [Akkik](../../npcs/akkik.md) heard the gossip and told Jabba within the hour. Jabba's people have been watching Docking Bay 94 ever since. They didn't need to chase the crew into the Wastes. Everyone has to come back to their ship eventually.

**How Jabba knows they can pay:** [Torel Vorne](../prologue-recap.md) has been telling everyone in the Outer Rim that this crew robbed him of ten thousand credits. Hutts listen to that kind of gossip. Bib's price matches what the crew has.

## The Scene

> *The docking bay doors grind open. Your ship sits where you left it, but someone is sitting on the boarding ramp. A tall, pale Twi'lek in dark robes rises slowly, head-tails sliding off his shoulder. Two Gamorreans with vibroaxes flank him. Neither of them looks at anyone but Torbek.*
>
> *"Ah. The heroes of the Mos Eisley police station." His smile shows too many teeth. "I am Bib Fortuna. I speak for His Excellency, Jabba the Hutt. And I am afraid you have something of his."*

### The Bill

Bib produces a datapad and reads from it in a pleasant, unhurried voice.

| Item | Bib's asking price | Floor after bargaining | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lost day's collections | 2,500 cr | **2,500 cr** | Not negotiable. *"It is His Excellency's money. Whether the Gamorrean lost it or ate it is immaterial."* |
| Buyout of Torbek's contract | 6,000 cr | **4,000 cr** | See the Reveal below. |
| "Inconvenience to His Excellency" | 1,000 cr | **0 cr** | Bib's padding. He gives it up if the crew bargains well. |
| **Total** | **9,500 cr** | **6,500 cr** | |

**Bargaining:** Opposed *bargain* (whoever negotiates) vs. Bib's **Bargain 6D+1**. For each step of success, take about 1,000 cr off the total, down to the 6,500 floor. A good argument (Torbek's years of service, the Empire about to land, the crew already being *in* the docking bay) gives the negotiator +1D. Threatening Bib gives +1D to *Bib*, because he enjoys it.

**Typical outcome:** about **7,500 cr**.

### The Reveal

When the crew asks why they should pay for Torbek's "contract," Bib smiles and reads it aloud.

> *"Bill of sale. One Gamorrean, male, of fighting age, transferred in full to the household of Jabba Desilijic Tiure… purchased from the clan of his birth…"* He glances up. *"For forty crates of Gamorrean ale. They drove a hard bargain, I'm told."*

Torbek was never an employee. His clan **sold him into slavery**, and Jabba has owned him ever since. The missing collections were only an excuse to come after him. The ownership was always the real hold.

**Let it land.** Henry was also rescued from slavery (by Gune). Play that parallel for all it's worth: Henry may move to stand next to Torbek without being asked.

## Outcomes

### They Pay
- Bib hands over the datapad. The bill of sale is now **Torbek's own**. He can destroy it, keep it, or give it to whoever paid.
- **Torbek is free.** For a Gamorrean, being bought out of bondage creates a serious debt of honor. Decide with the Torbek player who he feels he owes.
- Bib bows and leaves. *"His Excellency wishes you a pleasant journey. Wherever it is you are going in such a hurry."*
- Record the payment in the [Crew Ledger](../../README.md#crew-ledger) and on [Torbek's Rap Sheet](../../pcs/torbek.md#rap-sheet).

### They Pay Only the Collections (2,500 cr)
- Jabba considers the money matter settled. Torbek is still **property**, and now a runaway.
- Raise Torbek's Heat with **Jabba's organization** to ●●○ 2. Jabba posts a bounty on him (suggested 3,000 cr, alive) that follows him off Tatooine. Hutt contacts reach the Minos Cluster eventually.

### They Refuse Entirely
- Bib doesn't fight. He nods, steps aside, and the Gamorreans plant themselves at the bay doors.
- In the next scene, Jabba sells the crew's location to the Imperials. The stormtroopers arrive early and in larger numbers. Raise everyone's Mos Eisley Heat by one, and Torbek gets the Jabba bounty above.

### They Start Shooting
- Bib is the first one out the door. His Gamorreans cover his retreat.
- Same as refusing, except Jabba's bounty now covers the **whole crew** (500 cr each). Torbek's bounty is 5,000 cr, alive.
